PremiumFlights Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

1. Introduction

PremiumFlights ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Search Preferences: Departure/arrival airports, travel dates, cabin class, and passenger count
  • Price Alerts: Route and price threshold data for alerts you create
  • Saved Flights: Flights you bookmark or save for later
  • Trip Plans: Trip itineraries you create within the app

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Usage Data: App feature usage patterns (anonymized), search frequency, and crash reports
  • Network Status: Online/offline status for caching purposes only

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone) — no account required
  • Payment or financial information
  • Precise location data (we only use your general region for nearby airports if you grant permission)
  • Contacts, photos, or other personal data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To search for and display flight results based on your queries
  • To send price alert notifications you've opted into
  • To cache search results for offline access and faster loading
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs via anonymized crash reports
  • To display price trend predictions based on aggregated search data

4. Data Storage

All your data (saved flights, alerts, trip plans, preferences) is stored locally on your device using secure storage. We do not maintain user accounts or store personal data on external servers.

Crash reports and anonymized analytics may be sent to our error tracking service (Sentry) to improve app stability.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party APIs to provide flight data:

  • Amadeus: Flight search and pricing data
  • AviationStack: Real-time flight tracking data
  • ExchangeRate-API: Currency conversion rates
  • Sentry: Crash reporting and error tracking

These services receive only the search query data necessary to return results. No personal information is shared.

6. Push Notifications

We send push notifications only for price alerts you have explicitly created. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings or within the app.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all API communications
  • Secure on-device storage for sensitive data
  • Optional biometric authentication for app access
  • No collection of unnecessary personal data

8. Children's Privacy

PremiumFlights is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete all data: Use the "Clear All Data" option in Settings
  • Disable notifications: Through device settings or in-app
  • Disable location access: Through device settings
  • Uninstall the app: This removes all locally stored data

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
